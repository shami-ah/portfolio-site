## Content Principles

1. **Never say "I'm looking for a job"** -- show the work, let people reach out
2. **Every post links back to the portfolio** -- directly or through comments
3. **Technical depth > marketing speak** -- your audience is engineers and CTOs
4. **One post = one idea** -- don't bundle multiple topics
5. **Engage in comments** -- 50% of LinkedIn reach comes from comment activity in the first 2 hours
